Marine Biologist

A packaging design project developed for a highly specific target audience: marine biologists. Inspired by the microscopic world of marine life, the visual identity explores organic shapes, cellular structures, and the fluidity of water. Through an experimental analogue process using gouache, foam, and water, the design captures the textures and colours of microscopic imagery, translating them into a playful and expressive visual language.
