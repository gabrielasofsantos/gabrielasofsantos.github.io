Monstros Gregos

The project grew from the challenge of turning creatures from Greek mythology into physical objects while retaining their most recognisable features. The idea was to create a set of wooden pieces that could be combined in different ways to build both the original creatures and new imaginary figures. The pieces were designed digitally using vector illustration and produced in wood through laser cutting, resulting in a simple, interactive assembly system.
