O Sétimo Homem

A pocket book design inspired by O Sétimo Homem, a short story by Haruki Murakami that explores themes of loss, fear, and the haunting power of the sea. The cover translates the story’s emotional depth into a visual language of deep blues, ocean textures, and sea foam. Through expressive typography that gradually fades into the composition, the design evokes the feeling of disappearance and the overwhelming force of the ocean.
