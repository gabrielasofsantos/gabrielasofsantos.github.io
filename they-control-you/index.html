They Control You

An ESAD final-year project developed in 2026 around the theme “Extremes”, exploring the extreme of surveillance. The project resulted in a book that aims to raise awareness of the constant surveillance we are subject to, often without realising it. Reflective silver papers expose the reader’s face, reinforcing the feeling of a lack of privacy. By tearing the pages to discover what lies inside, readers themselves participate in the invasion of privacy that the project seeks to question.
