Vertigo

Vertigo is a visual identity and digital design project for an app dedicated to contemporary dance performances at Teatro Sá da Bandeira. The app allows users to explore shows, select seats and buy tickets online. Inspired by the movement and energy of dance, the visual identity explores fragmented typography and dynamic compositions, applied to both the interface and promotional posters.
