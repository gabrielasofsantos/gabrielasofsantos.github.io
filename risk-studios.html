Risk Studios

Risk Studios is a fictional video game development brand, originally created in 2022 as part of an academic project. This personal project revisits the brand identity by redesigning its logo and creating a new visual language. The identity was also applied to a website and a desktop app, designed to explore, discover and access the brand’s games.
