Error Report

The project began with the challenge of exploring the word “ERROR” through a physical medium. This led to Error Report, a fortnightly newspaper that approaches everyday mistakes and problems with humour and irony. With a visual identity entirely in shades of purple, the newspaper combines articles, crosswords and readers’ accounts. Readers can also suggest topics for future issues, creating a critical, playful and participatory publication.
