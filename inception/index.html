Inception

A typographic project inspired by Inception, exploring the construction and collapse of dreams through letterform. Developed from the idea of instability and fragmentation, the visual language translates the film’s shifting realities into cracked, textured typography that suggests both structure and disintegration. The project later expanded into a complete alphabet and a set of film credits, creating a cohesive graphic system rooted in the atmosphere and conceptual tension of the film.
