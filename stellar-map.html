Stellar Map

The project began with the challenge of creating a website inspired by Brian Cox’s video Facts about the Universe. The idea was to explore stars and constellations, turning the website into an interactive sky where visitors can discover and explore different constellations. Stars are represented in three colours according to their brightness, and each constellation can be selected to learn more about its stars, combining visual exploration and learning.
